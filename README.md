# FishingBucket
## Abstract
FishingBucket is a multi-platform bot that allows users to create and manage *proxies*, allowing themselves to chat as another profile.

This bot is currently available on [Fluxer](/fluxer) and [Discord](/discord).

![Demonstration of FishingBucket](/demo.gif)

Invites to the bot for each platform can be found on their respective pages.

## Motivation
The main motivation for FishingBucket's existence is the lack of a similar bot on the [Fluxer](/fluxer) platform. As Fluxer was quite new, a lack of such bot was guaranteed. However, due to the existence of similar bots on Discord, the demand for this type of service is ever-present.

The initial days of Fluxer's existence was marked with a variety of people advocating for such bot, and this situation was only made worse by waves of influxes of new users. Therefore, FishingBucket was established swiftly.

The absurd brand of FishingBucket and its neutrality of its use cases were established due to observed concerns with similar bots on Discord &mdash; Tupperbox was commonly seen as a roleplaying bot, and PluralKit was commonly seen as a plural bot. While those are all valid use cases, the segregation of use cases by the community was something that divided the global Discord proxy bot userbase.

FishingBucket aims to be the best proxy bot on its available platforms, unifying the previously-divided userbase of proxy bots. To achieve this, a primary objective of any new feature is to be as use case-agnostic and as general as possible.

## Features
A full list of the features of FishingBucket can be found [here](/features/index). The highlights can be found here!

### Proxies
Proxies are the heart and soul of the functionalities of FishingBucket. They are profiles that can send messages, which can be created, switched, and deleted easily.

### Triggers
Triggers are special patterns that messages are matched with to determine what to send through which proxy.

### Dashboard
There is a web dashboard! The web dashboard can be found [here](/../dashboard). The dashboard provides an intuitive way to manipulate user data.

### Moderation
FishingBucket also collaborates with the moderators of communities. The bot provides functionalities such as permissions and logging, helping to mitigate misuses of the bot.

### Tags
Tags are the primary way to organize proxies. A single proxy can have multiple tags. Tags can also affect the proxies that it contains in attributes like display name.

### Interactions
Beyond commands, FishingBucket also listens to other interactions like reactions, providing convenience.

### Autoproxy
Set a proxy to trigger on every message. There are many options to go with this feature, with modes like `latch`, `specific`, and `spotlight`.

### Spotlight
Show off one's proxies to the outside world (or keep them only for private). Give the spotlight of attention to a podium of proxies that were selected. This can be used to represent anything!
